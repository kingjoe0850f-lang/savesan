Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

SaveSan stores the account details you provide (name, email, date of birth, phone number) and the budgeting data you choose to add, so that your dashboard works across sessions. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, not plain text.

We use your email only for account verification and service-related messages. We do not sell personal data. You can request account deletion from SaveSan support.

Exchange-rate requests are made to ExchangeRate-API when you use currency conversion. Their handling of requests is governed by their own policy.
